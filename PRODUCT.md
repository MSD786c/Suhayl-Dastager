# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Potential employers and product collaborators, brands seeking UGC partnerships, and people evaluating Suhayl's founder work. They arrive to understand the person behind the work and decide whether to start a relevant conversation.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio for Suhayl Dastager that presents three equal bodies of work: founder and product work, creator and UGC work, and professional engineering and strategy work. Success is a visitor understanding all three as one credible career story and choosing the relevant way to collaborate.

## Positioning

One person operating across product building, technology-led content, and hands-on engineering, with proof from live companies, real creative work, and professional systems rather than separate personas or speculative claims.

## Operating Context

Visitors explore public project pages, founder narratives, UGC case work, professional experience, a resume, and a contact route. The site provides equal starting paths for Founder, UGC, and Work while maintaining a coherent overall narrative.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The project is a Next.js 16, TypeScript, Tailwind, and Framer Motion website with static portfolio pages and a contact API. Preserve supplied photography, UGC visuals, company and career information, existing route structure, working contact flow, real external links, SEO, and reduced-motion support. Do not invent clients, testimonials, metrics, partnerships, or brand claims.

## Brand Commitments

Use the established Suhayl Dastager personal identity and supplied SM Stratagem logo. The visual system should unite SM Stratagem's deep navy and electric blue with VoxxHire's indigo and coral in a credible, modern, minimal way. Avoid synthetic-looking AI/neon/3D logo aesthetics. Founder, UGC, and Work must receive equal visual weight across the experience.

## Evidence on Hand

Real portraits in `public/portrait/`; real UGC and client-brand assets in `public/ugc/`; project imagery in `public/project-pictures/`; SM Stratagem logo in `public/brand/sm-stratagem-logo.svg`; resume PDFs in `public/`; and factual copy and metrics in `lib/personal-brand.ts` and `lib/data.ts`.

## Product Principles

- Make the three identities feel like one career, not three disconnected brands.
- Let real work, real imagery, and supported outcomes earn credibility.
- Give every visitor an immediate, equal route to founder, creator, and professional work.
- Keep the interface calm enough for the work to speak.
- Build moments of motion around the narrative, not decorative spectacle.
